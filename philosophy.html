Daily philosophy.

Quotes, short summaries, and questions from my daily reading.

Habits shape character

“States of character arise out of like activities.”

Aristotle · Nicomachean Ethics, II.1 ↗ · W. D. Ross translation

Aristotle links character to repeated action. Patience, honesty, and courage grow through practice.

Question: What do your habits help you practice?

Learning and reflection

Confucius pairs learning with reflection. Test ideas against experience.

Question: What idea have you learned but not examined?

Read Analects, Book II · James Legge translation ↗

Know what you don’t know

In Plato’s Apology, Socrates asks whether expertise in one field makes us wise in others.

Question: What evidence would change your mind?

Read Plato’s Apology · Benjamin Jowett translation ↗
