PHILOSOPHY NOTEBOOK

A thought to sit with.

Selected ideas from my daily philosophy reading. A quote, some context, and a question.

What we practice, we become

“States of character arise out of like activities.”

Aristotle · Nicomachean Ethics, II.1 ↗ · W. D. Ross translation

Aristotle connects character to repeated choices. Patience, honesty, and courage take shape through practice, not just good intentions.

A question: What quality do your everyday habits give you the most practice in?

Learning needs reflection

In Analects 2.15, Confucius pairs learning with thinking. Taking in ideas matters, but so does examining them against experience.

A question: What idea have you recently absorbed without really thinking it through?

Read Analects, Book II · James Legge translation ↗

Knowing where knowledge ends

In Plato’s Apology, Socrates questions whether expertise in one field makes us wise about everything else. It is a useful question to bring to science, technology, and everyday life.

A question: What evidence would make you change a belief you feel sure about?

Read Plato’s Apology · Benjamin Jowett translation ↗
