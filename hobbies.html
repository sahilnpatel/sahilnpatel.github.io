OUTSIDE OF WORK

Passions & hobbies.

Music, movement, good company, and somewhere new.

Music

I’ve played clarinet since fourth grade, including All-County, jazz, and symphonic band. I’ve also spent time with guitar, and want to keep exploring piano and DJing. Music gives me a different way to express myself.

Tennis & movement

I started tennis in fifth grade and went on to play varsity singles and captain my high-school team. I also enjoy lifting, running, and beach volleyball. I completed the Tobay triathlon in 2023, and I like having something physical to work toward.

Getting outside

Hiking, trying new foods, and exploring unfamiliar places are some of my favorite ways to spend time with people. One long-term goal is to visit every national park.

Learning for the fun of it

I like picking up skills just because they look fun. I’ve learned to juggle, play poker, and shuffle cards; my ongoing list includes a handstand, a skateboard kickflip, and getting better at guitar.

People & perspective

Friends, family, and community are a big part of what makes life feel full to me. I enjoy conversations about philosophy and how people think, but also a good game, a laugh, and our fantasy-football league, which goes back to sixth grade.
