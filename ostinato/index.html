How the reading is calculated

The demo generates 360 samples over 30 seconds. It centers the available samples and tests frequencies from 6 to 40 cycles per minute in steps of 2. Peak power share is the strongest frequency's power divided by the total across those frequencies. The demo reports the strongest frequency when at least 65% of samples are visible and one frequency accounts for at least 55% of the power. These are demonstration thresholds.

Inside the research

Paired camera trials

The webcam protocol compares two positions with the same consenting participant. An observer counts breaths independently in each window. The analysis compares how often the camera reports a rate, the reference error in those readings, and how often the second view recovers a reading after the first withholds it. Read the evaluation plan.

What the simulation can show

The browser demo uses a generated signal and scripted geometry. It shows how a decision rule can withhold a reading and how the order of viewpoints changes elapsed time. Webcam performance needs paired observations. Read the evidence summary.
